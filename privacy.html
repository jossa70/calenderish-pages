Calenderish Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Calenderish is a calendar application under development. Contact: henrik.johansson1@gmail.com

Information we access

With your permission, Calenderish accesses your Google account identifier, name, email address, and Google Calendar events needed to manage events you select.

How we use your data

We use your information to authenticate you, manage event templates, and create, read, update or delete Google Calendar events at your request. We do not use Google data for advertising.

Data storage

Calenderish is being developed using PostgreSQL. Account data, application settings, event information and OAuth tokens may be stored to support its functionality. Security and retention details will be confirmed before public launch.

Data sharing

Calenderish communicates with Google APIs to perform requested operations. We do not sell Google user data.

Data deletion

To request deletion of your Calenderish data, contact henrik.johansson1@gmail.com. You can also revoke access through your Google Account. Revoking access does not automatically delete stored data.

Google API Services

Calenderish's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
