Privacy Policy

Calenderish — Last updated: November 3, 2026

Calenderish is a calendar planning application currently under development. This policy describes its intended use of Google account and Google Calendar data. The policy will be updated to reflect the actual implementation before broader availability.

Information accessed

With your permission, Calenderish requests basic Google account information (such as your name and email address) to identify your account, and Google Calendar event access to create, read, update, or delete calendar events that you manage through the application.

How information is used

Account information is used for sign-in and account management. Calendar access is used to synchronize events you choose to manage with Calenderish. Google user data is not used for advertising.

Storage and retention

Calenderish may need to store account identifiers, event references, application settings, and authorization tokens to provide its functionality. The exact storage and deletion practices will be documented here before the application is released.

Sharing

Calenderish does not intend to sell Google user data or share it with advertisers. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the application, subject to applicable protections.

Your choices

You can revoke Calenderish's Google account access at Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future authorized Google Calendar operations but does not automatically delete information already stored by the application.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application developer through the contact information provided when access to Calenderish is granted. A direct contact address will be added before public release.
